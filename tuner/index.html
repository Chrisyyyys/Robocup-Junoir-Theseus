Distance sensors

Hold a flat, matte target exactly mm in front of the sensor, then press Measure. It averages 100 raw readings and suggests the offset that makes the sensor read true. Apply sets it on the robot right away.

Color thresholds

  1. Put the sensor over a white tile and press Set white reference. All ratios are divided by this baseline. (The robot also measures it at boot, so power it on over a white tile.)
  2. Put the sensor over each tile type and press its Sample button (collects ~1.5 s of readings).
  3. Check the suggested thresholds, then Apply.
